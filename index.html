How it works

Four simple steps.

It all happens in the Trust Scan+ app.

  1. Get the app

    Download Trust Scan+ and create your account.

  2. Add your website

    Type in your website address.

  3. Prove it's yours

    A quick one-off step, so nobody else can check your website.

    See what we found and how to fix it. Then switch on monitoring.

Our servers do the checking, not your phone.

Proving it's your website

We only check websites that are yours.

Before we check anything, you prove the website belongs to you. This keeps you safe, and stops anyone using Trust Scan+ on a website that isn't theirs.

  1. 1

    Get your code. The app gives you a unique code for your website.

  2. 2

    Add it to your website. Your web developer or hosting company can do this for you in a few minutes.

  3. 3

    Tap verify. Once we find the code, you choose which checks we're allowed to run.

Warning type Advice Low Medium High Very high
  • BrowserBrowser protectionMedium

    Framing protection is absent

    Seen on 3 pages

    In plain English: another website could show your page inside its own and trick visitors into clicking things.

    Why it matters

    The page lacks framing protections. This can allow unwanted embedding; clickjacking was not demonstrated.

    What to do next

    Ask your developer to configure CSP frame-ancestors and, where appropriate, X-Frame-Options. Test intended embedding.

  • BrowserBrowser protectionLow

    HTTPS Transport Security header absent or empty

    Seen on 3 pages

    In plain English: browsers aren't being told to always use the secure version of your website.

    Why it matters

    Without this, a visit can start without encryption. This check did not establish an interception.

    What to do next

    Ask your developer or host to add Strict-Transport-Security once HTTPS and certificate renewal work reliably. Start with a short max-age and test before extending it.

  • BrowserBrowser protectionAdvice

    Enforcing CSP response header is absent

    Seen on 3 pages

    In plain English: your website doesn't tell browsers which code is allowed to run.

    Why it matters

    An enforcing CSP response header was not observed. This does not prove exploitable XSS.

    What to do next

    Ask your developer to design and test an enforcing CSP for the resources this page requires.

  • EmailEmail protectionAdvice

    No direct DMARC record observed

    Checked: brightbakes.example

    In plain English: there's no clear rule to stop fake emails that look like they come from your domain.

    Why it matters

    Email spoofing was not demonstrated. A direct DMARC record was not seen; a parent policy may apply.

    What to do next

    Ask your email provider to check any inherited policy and legitimate senders before proposing DMARC changes.

  • DNSDomain settingsAdvice

    No CAA policy observed

    Checked: brightbakes.example

    In plain English: you haven't limited which companies can issue security certificates for your website.

    Why it matters

    This scan did not establish certificate misuse. CAA is an optional restriction on which providers may issue certificates.

    What to do next

    Ask your DNS or hosting provider whether CAA would help your certificate setup. Check renewal before changing it.

Straight talk

What Trust Scan+ does, and what it doesn't.

What it doesn't do

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Can I check any website?

No. You can only check websites you've proved are yours.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Reports are written in plain English. When a fix needs a developer, we give you a short note to pass on.

Does it work when my phone is off?

Yes. The checks run on our servers, not on your phone. The app shows you the results and sends your alerts.

Will this make my website completely safe?

No tool can promise that. Trust Scan+ finds common problems and tells you when key settings change. It doesn't replace a full review by a security professional.

Who can see my reports?

Only you. Reports are private to your account. We're planning a safe way to share one, for example with your web developer.

How do I cancel or delete my account?

You'll manage your plan in the app. See account deletion for how to delete your account and data.

When can I get the app?

Soon. The download links will appear on this website as soon as the app is live.
