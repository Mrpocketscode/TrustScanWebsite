How it works

Four steps, all in the app.

Add your site, prove it's yours, and let our servers do the checking. You get a report you can understand and a list of things worth fixing.

  1. Add your website

    Sign in to the app and enter the exact web address you want checked.

  2. Prove you control it

    Follow the verification step and confirm which checks you give permission for.

  3. We run the checks

    Our scanning servers run the agreed checks. Your phone doesn't have to stay open.

  4. Read and fix

    See each finding, how serious it is and what to do about it. Then switch on monitoring.

Behind the scenes

The app is the remote control. Our servers do the work.

Checks don't run on your phone. They run on Trust Scan+ scanning servers, which is why monitoring keeps going while the app is closed.

  1. 1 · App

    You ask

    You request a report or switch on monitoring for a verified site.

  2. 2 · Account check

    We confirm

    We check you're signed in, have verified the site and have an active plan for it.

  3. 3 · Scanning server

    The engine runs

    Our scanning engine runs only the checks you've agreed to, against that exact hostname.

  4. 4 · Private report

    You see results

    Results are stored privately on your account and appear in the app, with your history.

  • Private by defaultReports belong to your account. There's no public results page.
  • Permission is recordedWe keep a record of each site you verified and the checks you agreed to.
  • Failures aren't hiddenIf a check can't finish, it's marked incomplete, never quietly shown as a pass.
Ownership verification

We only check websites you can prove are yours.

Before any check runs, you show that you control the exact hostname. It protects you, and it stops anyone using Trust Scan+ against a site that isn't theirs.

  1. 1

    Get a code in the app. Each website gets its own unique verification code.

  2. 2

    Place it on your site. For example, add it as a DNS record or upload a small file. You, your developer or your web host can do this.

  3. 3

    Tap verify. We look for the code on that exact hostname. Verifying example.com doesn't cover shop.example.com.

  4. 4

    Give permission. Confirm which checks we may run. We record it, and you can withdraw it at any time.

1 high 2 medium 2 low 8 passed 1 incomplete
  • HighSecurity certificate expires in 9 days

    What it means: Once the certificate expires, browsers will warn visitors that the site isn't secure, and most will leave.

    How to fix: Renew the certificate with your host, or turn on automatic renewal. Then run a new report to confirm it's fixed.

  • MediumNo Content-Security-Policy header

    What it means: Without this header, browsers have fewer defences if malicious script gets into a page.

    How to fix: Ask your developer to add a Content-Security-Policy, starting in report-only mode so nothing breaks.

  • MediumOld TLS 1.0 connections still accepted

    What it means: TLS 1.0 is an outdated version of the encryption that protects connections to your site, with known weaknesses.

    How to fix: Turn off TLS 1.0 and 1.1 in your hosting or CDN settings, keeping TLS 1.2 and 1.3.

  • LowServer software version is shown publicly

    What it means: Showing the exact version makes it easier for attackers to look up known problems with that software.

    How to fix: Hide the version number in your server's response headers.

  • LowNo Referrer-Policy header

    What it means: Full page addresses may be passed to other sites when visitors click links on yours.

    How to fix: Set Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin.

  • IncompleteEmail security records check

    What it means: The DNS lookup timed out, so we don't know the result. Incomplete checks are never counted as a pass.

    Next step: It will be tried again in the next report.

What to expect

What Trust Scan+ does, and what it doesn't.

We'd rather be clear than promise too much. Trust Scan+ gives you scheduled visibility into selected checks.

  • Does: run agreed, automated checks on websites you've verified, and explain the results.
  • Does: repeat lightweight checks on a schedule and tell you what changed.
  • Doesn't: block attacks or protect your site in real time.
  • Doesn't: check everything. No automated tool can guarantee a site is safe.
  • Doesn't: check websites you haven't verified, ever.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Can I check any website?

No. You can only check websites you have verified that you control, and only with the checks you've given permission for. Each hostname is verified separately.

Do I need an account to see the example report?

No. The examples on this page use invented data and need no account. To check your own site, you'll sign up in the app.

Does monitoring work when my phone is off?

Yes. Checks run on Trust Scan+ servers, not on your phone. The app shows results and sends notifications based on your settings.

Is this a penetration test?

No. Trust Scan+ runs automated checks you agree to beforehand. It isn't a replacement for a professional penetration test or a full security audit.

What happens if a check can't finish?

It's marked as incomplete, never shown as a pass. We try again at the next scheduled check.

Who can see my reports?

Your reports are stored privately on your account. We're planning a way to share or export a report safely, for example with your developer.

How do I cancel or delete my account?

You'll manage your subscription in the app. Cancellation details will be published before launch. See account deletion for how to delete your account and data.

When is the app available?

Soon. Store links will appear on this site as soon as the app is live. Until then, the app buttons take you to a coming-soon page.
