Proving it's your website

We only check websites that are yours.

Before we check anything, you prove the website belongs to you. This keeps you safe, and stops anyone using Trust Scan+ on a website that isn't theirs.

  1. 1

    Get your code. The app gives you a unique code for your website.

  2. 2

    Add it to your website. Your web developer or hosting company can do this for you in a few minutes.

  3. 3

    Tap verify. Once we find the code, you choose which checks we're allowed to run.

  • HighYour security certificate runs out in 9 days

    What it means: The certificate is what puts the padlock in the address bar. When it runs out, visitors see a warning that your website isn't secure, and most will leave.

    How to fix it: Ask your hosting company to renew it, or to switch on automatic renewal.

  • MediumA protection against harmful code is switched off

    What it means: Your website isn't telling browsers which code is safe to run, so harmful code is harder to block.

    How to fix it: Ask your web developer to switch this protection on.

    For your developer: add a Content-Security-Policy header.

  • MediumYour website still allows an old, weak type of connection

    What it means: Older connection types have known weaknesses that modern ones have fixed.

    How to fix it: Ask your hosting company to switch off the old types.

    For your developer: disable TLS 1.0 and 1.1.

  • LowYour website shows which software it runs on

    What it means: This makes it easier for someone to look up known problems with that software.

    How to fix it: Ask your web developer to hide the software version.

  • IncompleteWe couldn't finish the email protection check

    What it means: This check timed out, so we don't know the answer. We never count an unfinished check as a pass.

    What happens next: We'll try again in your next report.

Straight talk

What Trust Scan+ does, and what it doesn't.

We'd rather be honest than promise too much.

What it does

  • Checks websites you've proved are yours.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't block attacks or protect your website in real time.
  • It doesn't check everything. No tool can promise a website is completely safe.
  • It never checks a website you haven't proved is yours.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Can I check any website?

No. You can only check websites you've proved are yours.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Reports are written in plain English. When a fix needs a developer, we give you a short note to pass on.

Does it work when my phone is off?

Yes. The checks run on our servers, not on your phone. The app shows you the results and sends your alerts.

Will this make my website completely safe?

No tool can promise that. Trust Scan+ finds common problems and tells you when key settings change. It doesn't replace a full review by a security professional.

Who can see my reports?

Only you. Reports are private to your account. We're planning a safe way to share one, for example with your web developer.

How do I cancel or delete my account?

You'll manage your plan in the app. See account deletion for how to delete your account and data.

When can I get the app?

Soon. The download links will appear on this website as soon as the app is live.
