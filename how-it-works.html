How it works

Four simple steps, all in the app.

Add your website, prove it is yours, and our servers check it for you. You get a report you can understand, with clear steps to fix each problem.

  1. Get the app

    Download Trust Scan+ and create your account.

  2. Add your website

    Type in your website address.

  3. Prove it's yours

    A quick one-off step, so nobody else can check your website.

  4. Get your report

    See what we found and how to fix it. Then switch on monitoring.

Our servers do the checking, not your phone.

That's why monitoring keeps working when the app is closed. Your reports stay private to your account.

Proving it's your website

We only check websites that are yours.

Before we check anything, you prove the website belongs to you. This keeps you safe, and stops anyone using Trust Scan+ on a website that isn't theirs.

  1. 1

    Get your code. The app gives you a unique code for your website.

  2. 2

    Add it to your website. Your web developer or hosting company can do this for you in a few minutes.

  3. 3

    Tap verify. Once we find the code, you choose which checks we're allowed to run.

Example code trustscan-verification=7f3c-EXAMPLE-91ab

This is what a code looks like. Each website address is verified separately, and you can remove your permission at any time. The exact steps will be confirmed in the app before launch.
