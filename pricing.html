Straight talk

What Trust Scan+ does, and what it doesn't.

We'd rather be honest than promise too much.

What it does

  • Checks websites you've proved are yours.
  • Explains what it finds in plain English.
  • Checks again twice a day and tells you what changed.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't block attacks or protect your website in real time.
  • It doesn't check everything. No tool can promise a website is completely safe.
  • It never checks a website you haven't proved is yours.
